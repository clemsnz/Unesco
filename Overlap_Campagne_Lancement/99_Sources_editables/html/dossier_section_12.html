Every decision follows the logic of the course: target customer, problem, existing alternative, why it matters, evidence, riskiest assumption and next test. We separate what is documented in our project, what we propose, and what is only an objective. We do not have users, results or signed partners yet, and we do not claim any.

12.2 Launch positioning, promise and proof

Positioning. For 18–25-year-olds in Paris who already want to see their friends, Overlap is the planning app that first finds a time that works for the whole group, then a convenient place, so the plan finally leaves the group chat. Unlike a group chat, nobody has to scroll back to find who is free. Unlike a poll, nobody has to invent the dates.

Promise (French, for the public): « Ton “on se tient au jus” devient un vrai rendez-vous. » — Your “let's keep in touch” becomes a real date.

Proof = how the app works (from our “How it works” and our app mock-ups):

  • Overlap compares everyone's availability and suggests 2–3 common time slots; the group confirms a date in one click (screens “Calendar – Find a slot for everyone” and “Friendship Challenge – Here are 2 available slots”).
  • Only then, Overlap suggests convenient meeting places based on everyone's location (screen “Map”).
  • Reminders keep the plan alive until the day (screen “Drinks + Dinner – I'm in!”).

Product status. Overlap exists as mock-ups and a prototype. There is no app to download, so our calls to action are: register your group for the test evening and join the waitlist.

12.3 The creative idea

Our Canva opens with “We'll keep in touch! (on se tient au jus)”. Everybody says it, and it usually means: we will not see each other. The idea of the campaign is to show the exact moment a vague sentence becomes a real date. We add one word: “…jeudi ?”. It is the moment someone proposes a slot. Then the plan gets its day, time and place.

One visual system, in the order of the product: the chat bubble (vague) → the blue pill “jeudi ?” (the slot) → the black ticket Day / Time / Place (the plan). The slot always comes before the place, like in our interviews and in the app.

12.6 The launch event: « On se tient au jus… jeudi ? »

Why a group of friends would come. It is already an outing with your own friends (and the first juice is free). You test an app before everybody else. And you leave with something you never manage to get in the group chat: the date and the place of your next outing.

The key point. The evening already has a date and a place. So the activation is about the guests' NEXT outing. This is when they feel what Overlap does.

12.10 Measurement

North Star: number of activated groups, i.e. groups that confirmed a first outing.

What counts as an activated group — all four conditions:

  • at least 3 different people (the organiser + 2 friends) sent their availability with the group's link;
  • the group chose one slot (day + time) AND one place;
  • the date is within 14 days after the event;
  • at least 3 members confirmed it (vote on the night or “OUI” at D+1).

No double counting. Each group gets one ID at registration (the organiser's e-mail). People are identified by e-mail or phone, never by first name only. If two registrations share two thirds of the same people, they are merged. A group is counted once as “activated”; its later plans count as second outings.

Tools we really have: Tally (registration, availability, confirmations, all with the group code), one Google Sheet (group ID, members, availability count, slot, place, status D+1 / D+7 / D+14), Instagram and TikTok insights, Meta Ads Manager, WhatsApp replies logged by hand in the sheet. Owners: Hénora and Ines.

12.11 Overlap Free vs Overlap Premium

Rule 1: the core stays free. A group must be able to invite friends, find a common slot and choose a convenient place even if nobody, or only one person, pays. Otherwise our riskiest assumption (getting friends to join) becomes even harder.

Rule 2: one big reason to pay — the Rituals (“Rituels”). Premium users choose a rhythm for each group (e.g. once a month). Overlap watches the synced calendars and, before the group drifts apart, proposes the next 2 common slots, ready to confirm. It extends the “Friendship Challenge” already in our mock-ups (“It's been 45 days since you saw Thomas — here are 2 available slots”) from one reminder to an automatic habit.

Individual or group? The subscription is individual. When a Premium member organises a plan, the whole group benefits from that plan's Premium features (Ritual, multi-calendar availability). The friends stay free.

Camille's example. Camille has three circles: her childhood friends in Lyon, her Master's classmates and her colleagues at the agency, plus the new friends she meets at parties. With Free, she can plan any outing herself, for 3 groups. But the Lyon group only meets when someone thinks about it — and nobody does. With Premium, she sets a Ritual “La bande de Lyon – once a month”. Three weeks later, Overlap sends her: “2 créneaux communs : sam. 12 · 19h ou dim. 13 · 12h”. She taps once and forwards it. Her school and agency calendars are both synced, so the slots are real. For €3.99 a month — less than one drink — she stops being the one who always has to relaunch the group.

12.13 Sponsored places in the app

Ads must help the group, not interrupt it. We only accept native formats placed after the hard part (the slot) is done.

FormatWhere in the journeyFrequency
Sponsored place cardStep “Choose where to meet”, after the group confirmed its slot. Labelled « Sponsorisé » with a « Pourquoi ce lieu ? » button.Max. 1 per list of places, never in 1st position
Sponsored pinMap screen, “New places” tabMax. 1 highlighted pin visible at a time
Sponsored experience“Discover new experiences” feedMax. 1 card out of 6

Refused: pop-ups, videos, banners during slot finding, ads inside the invitations sent to friends, push notifications that sell something. A given venue is shown max. 2 times per user per week.

Accepted advertisers: places where groups meet in Paris — bars, cafés, restaurants, bowling alleys, escape games, karaoke, cultural venues. Refused: dating apps, gambling, tobacco and vaping, consumer credit, and any offer that pushes alcohol consumption (French Loi Évin: no “2 cocktails for 1”; we accept offers on food, tables or activities).

Relevance rules — a sponsored place is shown only if: it is open at the chosen slot; it accepts the group's size; it fits the group's budget level; no member's trip is more than 10 minutes longer than with the best non-sponsored place; it respects accessibility needs if someone set them. If no sponsored place passes these rules, none is shown.

Premium users never see sponsored places: only the ranking by travel time. If they open a partner venue themselves, its group offer is shown as information (“Offre partenaire”).

Recommendation for the launch: start with a 3-month free pilot for 4 to 6 founding partners, then use option C (paid placement billed per confirmed plan) for bars and restaurants, and option B (commission) only for venues that take bookings. Option A comes later, when we can show numbers. Why C first: the venue pays only for a result; the trigger happens inside Overlap, so we can prove it; and it works for a bar where groups arrive without booking.

How we check what triggers the bill.

  • Bar (walk-in): the trigger is a plan confirmed in Overlap (≥ 3 members, venue chosen, not cancelled before the slot). The venue receives each month a statement with plan IDs, dates and group sizes (no personal data). During the pilot we compare with the venue's own observations, and we test an optional “On est arrivés” check-in (QR at the counter) to measure how many confirmed plans really come.
  • Escape game (booked sessions): the trigger is a paid booking with the code OVERLAP in the venue's booking system. Each month we match their export with our list of codes; cancelled or refunded sessions are not billed.
