```
Create a 60-second, 16:9, 24 fps, photorealistic cinematic advertising film titled "THE MISSING CHAPTERS" for the UNESCO campaign "Education Starts Early" (student proposal, UNESCO × EFAP). Follow the attached storyboard (Image 1) exactly for order, timing and framing, and use Images 2–8 as strict visual references for the character, faces, costumes, sets, light and colours.

CONCEPT
A child's life is a storybook. School is Chapter 6. But no story starts at Chapter 6. A 6-year-old boy discovers that the first five chapters of his life-book have been torn out; each recovered page holds a printed photograph that comes alive and shows what he learned before school. Message: just because we don't count those years doesn't mean they don't count.

REFERENCE IMAGES
- Image 8: the hero boy at 6 and his classroom (face, curly hair, white school shirt, old hardcover book starting at Chapter 6 with torn stubs along the spine, teacher and classmates, window light).
- Image 7: top-down view of his desk, the book open at Chapter 6 and the five torn pages titled CHAPTER 1 to CHAPTER 5 on the terracotta floor in stripes of sunlight.
- Image 6: Chapter 1 memory (age 1, first steps toward his father, mother clapping).
- Image 5: Chapter 2 memory (age 2, washing hands with his mother).
- Image 1, tile S05: Chapter 3 memory (age 3, sharing a red wooden block with a little girl, caring adult behind).
- Image 4: Chapter 4 memory (age 4, magnifying glass, leaf, grandmother).
- Image 3: Chapter 5 memory (age 5, painting with an older girl).
- Image 2: the classroom atmosphere (sunlit Latin American first-grade classroom, teacher in an embroidered blouse, bunting).
- Image 1, tiles S08 and S09: the layout of the restored pages and the final message card.

CHARACTER — ALWAYS THE SAME CHILD
Latin American boy, dark brown curly hair, warm light-brown skin, brown eyes, round soft cheeks, expressive eyebrows. Present day (age 6): white short-sleeved school shirt, puzzled then moved. In every memory (ages 1, 2, 3, 4, 5) he wears a small royal-blue graduation cap with a gold tassel and a royal-blue cape trimmed with gold, and he ages plausibly from baby to 5-year-old with the same face. The mother (curly dark hair, gold hoop earrings, embroidered white blouse), the father, the grandmother (grey hair, embroidered blouse) and the older girl (long dark braid) must match the references.

KEY VISUAL DEVICE — "THE LIVING PHOTOGRAPH" (shots 3 to 7)
Each memory appears inside a physical, aged cream paper page with torn edges, the coloured "CHAPTER" header seen in Image 7 (1 green, 2 blue, 3 magenta, 4 orange, 5 purple) and a rectangular printed photograph. The photograph starts as a still, slightly faded sepia print for one second, then softly develops into full colour and comes alive. Only the image inside the photograph moves; the paper, its torn edges, its margins and its printed header stay perfectly still, sharp and real, lit by the same warm window light. The moving image never leaves the rectangle: no hologram, no screen, no full-screen flashback. The camera slowly pushes in so the living photograph fills most of the frame while paper margins remain visible.

SHOT LIST (exact timing)
SHOT 1 — 0:00–0:08 — THE INCOMPLETE BOOK (Image 8). Sunlit classroom. The boy opens his old hardcover storybook: it starts at Chapter 6, "My First Day at School". He turns back and finds only ragged torn stubs where earlier pages were ripped out; he runs his fingertips over them, frowning, confused and curious. Classmates and the smiling teacher softly blurred behind. Camera: intimate close-up, slow dolly-in on his face and the torn stubs. Sound: soft classroom murmur, book cover opening, paper.

SHOT 2 — 0:08–0:15 — THE DISCOVERY (Image 7). He looks down under his desk: five torn cream pages lie scattered on the terracotta tiles, their edges glowing in golden stripes of sunlight, dust floating. His small hand reaches down and carefully picks up the CHAPTER 1 page; the paper bends naturally. Camera: follows his gaze from the desk to the floor, top-down to low floor-level angle, shallow depth of field, ending on the page in his hands. Sound: paper rustle, quiet room.

SHOT 3 — 0:15–0:22 — CHAPTER 1: FIRST STEPS (Image 6). Close-up of the CHAPTER 1 page (green header). The photograph comes alive: the 1-year-old boy in his blue cap and cape takes his first wobbly steps across a tiled living-room floor toward his father's open hands, arms out for balance, almost falls, keeps going and laughs; his mother kneels behind, clapping. Emotion: tenderness, encouragement. Sound: baby laughter, mother's soft cheer, soft piano begins.

SHOT 4 — 0:22–0:29 — CHAPTER 2: TAKING CARE (Image 5). Gentle page-turn to the CHAPTER 2 page (blue header). Inside the photograph: the 2-year-old boy rubs foamy soapy hands under a running tap, copying his smiling mother beside him; he splashes, giggles, and tries again more carefully. Emotion: pride, autonomy. Sound: running water, giggles.

SHOT 5 — 0:29–0:36 — CHAPTER 3: LEARNING TO SHARE (Image 1, tile S05). Page-turn to the CHAPTER 3 page (magenta header). Inside the photograph: the 3-year-old boy at a small table holds a red wooden block against his chest, hesitates for a second, then offers it to a little curly-haired girl; she beams, they build together; a caring adult smiles in the background. Emotion: empathy, connection. Sound: children's laughter, wooden blocks.

SHOT 6 — 0:36–0:43 — CHAPTER 4: DISCOVERING THE WORLD (Image 4). Page-turn to the CHAPTER 4 page (orange header). Inside the photograph: in a lush green garden, the 4-year-old boy looks through a magnifying glass at a large leaf held by his grandmother; a small red ladybug crawls across the leaf; his eyes widen with wonder; leaves sway in dappled sunlight. Emotion: wonder, curiosity. Sound: birds, light breeze.

SHOT 7 — 0:43–0:50 — CHAPTER 5: CREATING TOGETHER (Image 3). Page-turn to the CHAPTER 5 page (purple header). Inside the photograph: on a sunny patio, the 5-year-old boy and an older girl paint a sun, a house and flowers on a large white sheet; they dip brushes in shared paint pots, mix colours, exchange a brush and laugh together. Emotion: joy, cooperation. Sound: brush strokes, laughter, music swells gently.

SHOT 8 — 0:50–0:54 — EVERY CHAPTER COUNTS (Image 1, tile S08; classroom from Image 8). Back in the classroom, the 6-year-old boy lays the five recovered pages in order on his wooden desk, then slides them back into the front of his book before Chapter 6 and slowly turns through his now complete story; he closes the book, rests his hands on it and smiles softly. Camera: overhead, then slow steady pull-back. Emotion: quiet resolution. Sound: pages turning, piano.

SHOT 9 — 0:54–1:00 — CAMPAIGN LINE + SIGNATURE (Image 1, tile S09). Soft dissolve to a clean, warm ivory paper-textured background, calm and minimal. Space reserved, centred, for the final text (to be added in editing): "Just because we don't count those years doesn't mean they don't count." then "THE YEARS WE DON'T COUNT." and "Education Starts Early". Optional warm, natural adult voice-over (English), calm and sincere, at 0:54: "Just because we don't count those years doesn't mean they don't count." Music resolves and fades out at 1:00.

VISUAL STYLE
Premium international advertising quality, photorealistic, warm late-afternoon sunlight, soft shadows, gentle highlight roll-off, high dynamic range, natural skin and fabric textures, natural wood, terracotta tiles, aged cream paper, shallow depth of field, subtle 35mm film grain. Palette: warm ivory, cream, honey, muted amber, natural wood, soft sage green, gentle earth tones, restrained royal-blue accents. Camera: slow dolly-ins, gentle tracking, macro close-ups on paper and hands, controlled handheld micro-movements, soft focus pulls. Transitions: soft dissolves and natural page-turns only. Diverse, joyful, dignified, never sad or pitiful.

SOUND
Intimate original solo piano with soft ambient textures, starting almost silent and growing warmer at each chapter; realistic foley (book, paper, water, birds, laughter); no lyrics; no dialogue except the optional final voice-over.

STRICT RULES
Same boy and same family in every shot; consistent classroom, desk, book and page design; realistic anatomy and hands (five fingers), natural child movements, no facial distortion, no morphing. The memories play only inside the photograph rectangles. No subtitles, no watermark, no logo (the official UNESCO logo will be added in editing), no extra text beyond the CHAPTER headers already printed on the pages. No cartoon look, no fast zooms, no flashy transitions, no lens-flare overload, no oversaturation.
```

## Si l'outil limite la durée (8 à 10 s par génération)
1. Envoyez d'abord le prompt complet ci-dessus, pour que l'IA ait le contexte.
2. Générez ensuite plan par plan : copiez le paragraphe **SHOT n**, suivi des sections **CHARACTER**, **KEY VISUAL DEVICE**, **VISUAL STYLE** et **STRICT RULES**, et joignez l'image de référence du plan.
3. Assemblez les plans dans Canva ou CapCut avec des fondus de 0,5 s.
4. Ajoutez la carte finale et le logo officiel au montage.
