# Integrating the campaign into our two group files

This session could not edit the Google Doc or the Canva directly (no Google Drive or Canva connector, and both sites are blocked by the session's network). The two files below are ready to import.

## 1. Google Doc — `Overlap_Marketing_Communication.docx`
Section title: **MARKETING & COMMUNICATION** (parts I to X), same layout as the rest of the dossier: A4, 2.54 cm margins, Arial 11, bold titles « I. », « A. », « 1. », figures next to the text that explains them.
1. In Google Drive: New → File upload → `Overlap_Marketing_Communication.docx`, then open it with Google Docs.
2. Select all (Ctrl/Cmd + A), copy.
3. In our dossier, place the cursor where section 12 « Marketing & Communication » goes (after « LEGAL STATUS & DATA PROTECTION », before the roadmap) and paste. If a draft already exists there, paste under it and merge, then delete the duplicate lines.

## 2. Canva — `Overlap_Marketing_Communication.pptx` (22 slides, 1440 × 810, like our Canva)
1. In Canva: Create a design → Import file → `Overlap_Marketing_Communication.pptx`. Titles and text stay editable.
2. In the imported design, open the page grid, select all 22 pages, copy.
3. In our Canva, click the « Marketing & Communication » divider (page 24) and paste: the new slides become **pages 25 to 46**.
4. Check that titles use Monument Extended and text uses Syne (Canva has both). The speaker notes are in the PPTX notes.
