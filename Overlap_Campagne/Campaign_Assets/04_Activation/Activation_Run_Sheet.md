# Activation « Le Mur des Semaines Pro » — run sheet

**Where:** EFAP Paris, main hall near the cafeteria (to be approved by the school administration — request sent by Agathe in the week of 18 Nov).
**When:** Tue 1 Dec and Thu 3 Dec 2026, 12:00–14:00 (lunch break, when groups of classmates move together). Set-up 11:15, pack-up 14:15.
**Partnerships envisaged (not secured):** EFAP administration (space, required) · EFAP student union / BDE (relay of the C3 story, optional). No bar or brand partnership in the pilot, so free drinks do not bias the meet-up rate.

## Scene, from a participant's point of view
Léa (21, M1) walks to the cafeteria with two classmates. A big board says « Le Mur des Semaines Pro ». Clémentine asks: « Depuis quand ton groupe dit "la semaine pro" ? ». Léa laughs: « depuis septembre ». She writes « Les filles de L3, depuis septembre » on an orange post-it and sticks it in the "plan graveyard". Opale hands the three of them a transparent week grid each: they cross out the evenings they are busy, then stack the three sheets on the table. One cell stays clear: Thursday 10 Dec, 20:00–22:00. They fill a « C'est calé » card, take a photo and send it to their group chat. Léa's two other friends weren't there: she signs the whole group up for the pilot on Hénora's tablet (1 minute). That evening the planning group asks for their free evenings; two days later a poll with 3 common slots arrives; the five vote; the date is locked.
