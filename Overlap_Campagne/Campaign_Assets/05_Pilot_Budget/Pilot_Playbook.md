# Overlap — pilot playbook (concierge test, 1–18 Dec 2026)

Overlap is still a prototype (screens in our Canva), so the pilot is a **concierge / "Wizard of Oz" test**, as planned in our working document (Workshop 2, "Next test" n°2): the team plays the app by hand on one shared WhatsApp number. Nobody downloads anything. The test checks the group behaviour (do friends answer, does a date get locked, do people really meet), not calendar sync.

## Flow for one group
1. **Sign-up (organiser, 1 min)** — at the stand (tablet, Google Form) or by replying « GROUPE » to the story. We note: first name, group name, group size, source (poster / stand / Instagram / friend), and the baseline question: *"Of the last 3 plans you tried to make in this group chat, how many actually happened?"* Consent box + privacy notice (Agathe).
2. **Planning group** — the pilot number creates a temporary WhatsApp group « Overlap · [group name] » and sends the invite link to the organiser. The organiser forwards it in the usual chat with the message below. (We never join the friends' own group chat.)
3. **Free times (friends, 30 s)** — each friend joins with one tap and sends their free evenings for the window.
4. **Slots (within 12 h)** — a team member compares the answers and posts a native WhatsApp poll with the **2–3 slots when everyone is free**.
5. **Lock** — when every member has voted for the same slot, the date is **confirmed**. We post the « C'est calé » card + a convenient place (halfway between members' metro stations).
6. **Reminders** — the day before and 2 h before.
7. **Check-in (morning after)** — poll « Vous vous êtes vus ? Oui, tous / Oui, pas tous / Non ». **Met** = at least 2 members, including one who is not the organiser, answer yes.
8. **Close** — group closed after the meetup; data deleted by 31 Jan 2027.

If nobody has answered after 24 h, one reminder. If after 48 h one person still blocks, we ask the group if they want to lock the date without them (logged as "quorum rule", not counted as a full confirmation).

## Message scripts (French, as sent) — English in italics
**Organiser → usual group chat**
> Les gens, ça fait 3 semaines qu'on dit « la semaine pro ». J'ai inscrit le groupe au pilote Overlap : rejoignez ce petit groupe (1 tap) et envoyez juste vos soirs libres d'ici le 18/12. Ils nous renvoient 3 créneaux où on est TOUS libres, on vote, c'est calé. [lien du groupe]
*Guys, we've been saying "next week" for 3 weeks. I signed us up for the Overlap pilot: join this small group (1 tap) and just send your free evenings until 18/12. They send back 3 slots when we're ALL free, we vote, done.*

**Pilot number → planning group (welcome)**
> Salut ! Ici Overlap (version test). Envoyez vos soirs libres d'ici le 18 déc., ex : « mar, jeu après 19h, sam midi ». 30 secondes. Dès que tout le monde a répondu, on vous propose 2 à 3 créneaux où vous êtes tous libres. Vos réponses ne servent qu'à ça et seront effacées après le pilote.
*Hi! This is Overlap (test version). Send your free evenings until 18 Dec… As soon as everyone has answered, we suggest 2–3 slots when you are all free. Your answers are only used for this and deleted after the pilot.*

**Poll**
> 3 créneaux où vous êtes TOUS libres. Votez pour le vôtre : Mar. 8 déc. 20h / Jeu. 10 déc. 19h30 / Sam. 12 déc. 12h
*3 slots when you are ALL free. Vote for yours.*

**Lock**
> C'est calé ✓ Jeudi 10 déc., 19h30, 5/5. Lieu pratique pour tout le monde : [bar] (12 à 18 min pour chacun). On vous rappelle la veille.
*Locked in ✓ Thursday 10 Dec, 7:30 pm, 5/5. Convenient place for everyone… We'll remind you the day before.*

**Reminder (day before / 2 h before)**
> Demain 19h30, c'est vous. Toujours bon ? 👍 / Dans 2 h ! [bar]
*Tomorrow 7:30 pm is your plan. Still on? / In 2 hours!*

**Check-in (morning after)**
> Alors, vous vous êtes vus ? Oui, tous / Oui, pas tous / Non. (Une photo si vous voulez, pas obligatoire.)
*So, did you meet? Yes, everyone / Yes, not everyone / No.*

## Data collected (Google Sheet = `Overlap_Budget_and_Pilot_Tracker.xlsx`, tab "Pilot tracker")
Group ID, source, size, baseline, sign-up date, invite sent, friends invited, friends who replied within 48 h, poll date, date confirmed, hours to confirmation, met, people present, check-in answers. The KPI block at the bottom computes every rate automatically.

End survey (5 questions, all participants): Would this meetup have happened without Overlap? · What almost made you not answer? · Would you share your calendar (free/busy only) with an app to skip step 3? · Would you use it again with another group? · Who else should we invite?
Plus 5 short calls with groups that did not lock a date ("What happened?").
