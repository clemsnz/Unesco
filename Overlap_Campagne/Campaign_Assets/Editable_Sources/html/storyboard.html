OverlapFilm storyboard · Campaign « La semaine pro »

« 47 messages »
a 28-second vertical film

A group chat says « on se capte la semaine pro » and stalls. Camille moves the group to Overlap; her friends answer in 30 seconds, one slot gets 5/5 and the plan is locked. Thursday, 19:30, they actually meet.

Format9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920, 28 s (8 shots). Subtitled: every line is on screen, works without sound. Shot withOne smartphone (4K 30 fps), handheld + phone screen recordings of the prototype screens. Edit in CapCut (free). CastFive friends (team members or volunteers, written consent for image use): Camille, Léo, Nora, Sarah, Yanis. LocationsMetro or bus seat (shot 3), office desk, library, street (shot 5), a bar terrace near campus with the owner's permission (shot 7). ScreensIllustrative prototype screens (same as social post C2). They show the target product; during the pilot the same steps run by message. MusicRoyalty-free track from the CapCut / Instagram library. No commercial song. PostingInstagram Reel + TikTok, Tue 1 Dec, 8 pm. Also played on a loop on a laptop at the stand. Shoot daySat 28 Nov, about 3 hours. Lead: Francesca. Props budget: €25.
